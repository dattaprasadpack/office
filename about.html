About Us

Datta Prasad Packaging manufactures wooden pallets and provides industrial packaging, palletization and export packing services from Navi Mumbai, serving businesses since 2013.

to Your World
Who We Are

Your Trusted Partner in Palletization & Export Packaging

Datta Prasad Packaging is a reliable provider of wooden pallets, industrial packaging and material-handling solutions. We are committed to delivering high-quality, durable and cost-effective products that help businesses safely store, handle and transport their valuable goods.

From standard pallets and custom crates to palletization, export packing and container lashing, we handle every step of getting your cargo ready to move, for domestic dispatch and international shipments alike.
